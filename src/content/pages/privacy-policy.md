# Privacy Policy

Version: `v1.1.0`
Last updated: `2026-04-18`

---

## 1. Overview

PickleGo ("we", "our", or "us") provides a platform for users to create and join pickleball matches.

This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

---

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Device & Session Data

- Device identifiers
- Session tokens
- App and device metadata

---

### 2.2 Profile & Activity Data

- Display name and profile photo
- Match participation (create/join)
- Messages between users
- Reports and moderation activity

---

### 2.3 Location Data

- Approximate or precise location (based on user permission)

Used to:

- Show nearby matches and courts
- Improve match discovery relevance

We do not continuously track background location.

---

### 2.4 Date of Birth (Age Verification)

- Date of birth collected during onboarding

Used to:

- Verify eligibility (18+ requirement)
- Enforce platform access restrictions

We do not publicly display date of birth.

---

### 2.5 Phone Verification (Optional)

- Phone number used for one-time verification

Raw phone numbers are not stored in primary application data systems.
Verification status is stored as tokenized or hashed metadata.

---

### 2.6 Legal Acceptance Records

- Policy version accepted
- Timestamp
- Device/session metadata

---

### 2.7 Public Data

The following may be visible to other users:

- Display name and profile photo
- Match participation
- Reviews or feedback (if applicable)

---

## 3. How We Use Data

We use data to:

- Provide and operate the platform
- Enable match creation, discovery, and communication
- Enforce age restrictions and eligibility
- Detect and prevent abuse
- Enforce Terms and Community Guidelines

We do not sell user data.

---

## 4. Data Sharing

### 4.1 With Other Users

- Profile and match-related information
- Messages within matches

### 4.2 With Service Providers

We use infrastructure providers (e.g., hosting, database services) to operate the platform.

### 4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose data if required by law or to protect users and platform integrity.

---

## 5. Data Retention

We retain data as long as necessary to:

- Provide the service
- Enforce rules and prevent abuse
- Comply with legal obligations

Some data may be retained after account termination for safety and compliance purposes.

---

## 6. Security

We implement reasonable safeguards to protect data.

However, no system is completely secure.

---

## 7. User Rights

Users may request:

- Access to their data
- Correction of inaccurate data
- Deletion of their data

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

PickleGo is strictly limited to users aged 18 and above.

We do not knowingly collect or retain data from minors.
If we discover such data, we may delete it and terminate access.

---

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

---

## 10. Contact

Contact us through official support channels for privacy concerns.
