# Request Account Deletion

Last updated: **April 2026**

You can request deletion of your PickleGo account and associated data at any time using the form below.

## How to Request Deletion

To delete your account, submit a request using this link: [Submit Deletion Request](https://forms.gle/AhrQYEGSFsEfQDtZ6).

Please provide the email or account information associated with your PickleGo account so we can process your request.

## What Will Be Deleted

- Your profile information (name, photo)
- Match history and participation records
- Messages and chat data
- Account-related activity data

## What May Be Retained

Some information may be retained where necessary for:

- Legal compliance
- Fraud prevention and abuse detection
- Safety and moderation records

## Processing Time

Account deletion requests are typically processed within **7 days**.

## Important Notes

- Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone
- You may lose access to matches, messages, and history
- You may be required to verify ownership of the account
